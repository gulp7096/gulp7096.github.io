Book a repair: All World Automotive

Pick a service and a day. The shop takes as many cars as its bays can handle that day, and no more. Once your car is in, you get a text at every stage instead of having to call and ask.

Concept demo, not All World’s booking system. This page shows how capacity-aware booking and status texts could work for All World, using their real services and hours (Tue–Sat, 8am–4pm). The bay and technician counts below are illustrative assumptions; the real numbers are locked with the owner before anything goes live. All World has not endorsed this demo.

How capacity works (nothing hidden)

Demo assumptions: 3 bays, 2 technicians, maximum 6 cars per day. A booking counts against the day it is made for. When the day is full, the system says so and offers the next open day instead of taking a car the shop cannot get to. In the real system these numbers come from the shop. The demo shows the mechanism.
