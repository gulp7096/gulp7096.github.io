Instant quote: L&G Cleaning Services

Pick a service and tell us about your space. Standard home cleans get a firm quote and a confirmed appointment on the spot. Complex jobs go to Luis for a personal confirmation, as they do today.

Concept demo, not L&G’s booking system. This page shows how automated quoting could work for L&G, using their published price ranges. The quote rules below are illustrative; exact pricing is locked with the owner before anything goes live. L&G has not endorsed this demo.

How the quote is calculated (nothing hidden)

Base price starts at the low end of the service’s published range, then adds $35 per bedroom beyond the first and $25 per bathroom beyond the first. The home-size adjustment ($20 for 1,500–2,500 sq ft, $40 over 2,500 sq ft) is included inside that cap, so the base clean never exceeds the published high end. Add-ons are flat fees on top, shown as their own line items. The frequency or senior discount applies last. In the real system these rules are locked with Luis; here they are illustrative.
